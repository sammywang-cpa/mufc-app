📖 User Manual
COMPETITION FORMAT
Teams foil competition. Teams are generated by the app — even number of teams, each with 3 or 4 fencers, with at least one Int/Adv and one Beginner/Novice per team where possible. Teams are balanced by total worth score.

Standard Direct Elimination (DE) — seeded by team strength, with byes given to top seeds. Everyone gets at least 2–3 matches. Finals are for placement.
Round structure
Round 1 (+ byes for top seeds)30 pts
Round 2 / Quarter-Finals30 pts
Semi-Finals45 pts
🥉 Bronze Final — 3rd place45 pts
🥇 Gold Final — 1st place45 pts
Seeding & byes
Teams seeded by worth score (Beginner=1, Novice=2, Int/Adv=3). Teams with equal worth are randomly drawn. Bracket size = next power of 2 — top seeds get R1 byes so strong teams don't meet until later rounds.
4 teams → 4-bracket 6–8 teams → 8-bracket (2 byes) 10–16 teams → 16-bracket
Bout rules (relay format)
Fencers rotate each bout. Bout ends when running total hits milestone or 3 minutes passes. Scores carry over. Int/Adv fences Bout 1 and Bout 6 (first and last).
B1→5 · B2→10 · B3→15 B4→20 · B5→25 · B6→30 B7→35 · B8→40 · B9→45
Rankings for eliminated teams (FIE)
Ranked by: 1. Deepest round reached  2. Point differential (scored − conceded)  3. Total points scored
Estimated time: ~70–90 min for up to 16 teams across 5 pistes.
TEAM GENERATION
Beginner = 1  ·  Novice = 2  ·  Int/Advanced = 3
Teams are balanced by total worth. Each team aims for at least one Int/Adv and one Beginner/Novice. 3–4 fencers per team, no one left out. First-round bracket seeded by closest worth.
👁 SPECTATOR
Default if no role is set. View all matches, bracket, and standings. Cannot score, register, or edit anything.
🤺 COMPETITOR
Committee pre-registers your name before the event. On competition day:

First time → tap Check in, select your name from the list, set a 4-digit PIN.
Already checked in → tap Log in, select your name, enter your PIN.

Once logged in:
• View all matches, bracket, and standings
• Your bouts are highlighted ★ in gold
• Tap any match to view bout details and scores
• Edit your team name
⚖ REFEREE — PIN required
• Score bouts — add or undo points
• Issue / undo yellow and red cards (🟥 = +1 to opponent)
• Start, pause, reset, or manually set the bout timer
• Swap fencers within the bout order
• Edit scores on any completed bout
• End bouts and confirm or dispute match results
🛡 COMMITTEE — PIN required
Full override — all referee functions, plus:
• Register fencers before the event (name + level) — competitors cannot add themselves
• Remove any fencer from the register
• Reset a competitor's check-in if they used the wrong name
• Generate teams and draw, start the competition
• Edit event info (date, venue, time)
• Edit bout scores even after a winner is declared
• Change individual competitor PINs or clear all competitor data
• Change referee and committee PINs
