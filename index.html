📖 User Manual
COMPETITION FORMAT
Teams relay event — Foil. Two teams fence in relay. Fencers rotate each bout. First team to reach the match target wins.

30 pts (5+ teams) — 6 bouts  |  45 pts (≤4 teams) — 9 bouts

Each bout has a 5-point target milestone. A bout ends when either the running total hits that milestone or 3 minutes passes — whichever comes first. Scores carry over into the next bout.
Bout 1→ 5 pts
Bout 2→ 10 pts
Bout 3→ 15 pts
Bout 4→ 20 pts
Bout 5→ 25 pts
Bout 6→ 30 pts
Bout 7→ 35 pts
Bout 8→ 40 pts
Bout 9→ 45 pts
Bouts 7–9 apply to the 45-point format only.
TEAM GENERATION
Beginner = 1  ·  Novice = 2  ·  Int/Advanced = 3
Teams are balanced by total worth. Each team aims for at least one Int/Adv and one Beginner/Novice. 3–4 fencers per team, no one left out. First-round bracket seeded by closest worth.
👁 SPECTATOR
Default if no role is set. View all matches, bracket, and standings. Cannot score, register, or edit anything.
🤺 COMPETITOR
Register once with name, level, and a 4-digit PIN. Sign in from any device.
• Edit your own team name
• View all matches, bracket, and standings
• Your bouts are highlighted ★ in gold
• Tap any live or completed match to view bout details
⚖ REFEREE — PIN required
• Score bouts — add or undo points
• Issue / undo yellow and red cards (🟥 = +1 to opponent)
• Start, pause, reset, or manually set the bout timer
• Swap fencers within the bout order
• Edit scores on any completed bout
• End bouts and confirm or dispute match results
🛡 COMMITTEE — PIN required
Full override — all referee functions plus:
• Generate teams and start the competition
• Edit event info (date, venue, time)
• Remove any fencer from the register
• Edit bout scores even after a winner is declared
• Change referee and committee PINs
• Clear all competitor PINs
